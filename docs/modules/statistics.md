# Statistics

The statistics give an overview of the content of the graph: how many instances exist per type and how well they
comply with their validation schemas.

!!! note
    The figures only cover the spaces you can read.

## Validation

For every validation schema, a bar shows the live instances it applies to by their compliance - from fully compliant
to not compliant. *Show the compliance per type* breaks these figures down by type, the type with the most instances
first.

While all instances are being validated (e.g. after a validation schema has changed), a progress bar shows how many of
them are done. The figures are updated once the validation has finished.
